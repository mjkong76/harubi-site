A budget app that reads your receipts, not your bank account

Harubi is a free expense tracker for iPhone. Photograph a receipt, a statement screen, or a card approval text message, and it reads the date, amount and merchant on your device. No bank login. No account. No subscription.

iPhone only · iOS 17 or later · Free, with no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads

Why photograph anything?

Most budgeting apps in the US ask for two things before they will do anything: a monthly fee and your online banking credentials. YNAB, Copilot, Monarch and Rocket Money all work that way. That arrangement is fine until one of three things is true for you:

  • You pay cash sometimes. A bank feed cannot see cash. The farmers market, the barber, splitting a bill — none of it shows up, and the categories quietly drift away from reality.
  • You would rather not hand over banking credentials. A photograph asks nothing of your bank.
  • You do not want another account. Harubi has no sign-up screen because there is no server to sign up to.

A photograph covers all three. Everything you spend leaves a paper or a pixel trail: a receipt in your pocket, the transaction list in your banking app, the approval text that arrives seconds after you tap your card. Harubi's job is to turn that into a ledger entry so you do not have to type it.

It recognises three kinds of image, automatically

You do not pick a mode. Harubi works out what it is looking at.

Receipts

Store, restaurant and grocery receipts — date, total and merchant name. This is how cash spending gets recorded.

Bank & card statements

Screenshot the transaction list in your banking or card app and Harubi reads many rows in one pass.

Card approval texts

A screenshot of the confirmation message your card issuer sends after a purchase — amount, merchant and time.

Several screenshots, merged into one clean list

A month of transactions never fits on one screen. So you scroll and capture three or four screenshots, and they always overlap. Hand all of them to Harubi at once and it will:

  • Remove the duplicates by matching running balances, so an overlapping transaction is not imported twice.
  • Carry the date down to rows that do not show one, so a capture that starts mid-list still lands on the right day.
  • Untick anything you have already saved, so you can re-import a range you partly captured last week without thinking about it.

This is the part that makes catching up on a whole month bearable, and it is the thing no receipt scanner we know of does.

Nothing is saved until you have looked at it

What Harubi reads goes to a review screen first, never straight into your ledger. Tap any amount, description, date or category to correct it, and untick rows you do not want. Share a screenshot to Harubi from another app and it will be waiting for you the next time you open Harubi.

On-device, and that is not a marketing word

  • Recognition uses Apple's Vision framework on your iPhone. There is no recognition server, no cloud OCR API, no upload for recognition.
  • No account and no bank connection. Nothing to breach, because nothing is held.
  • Zero analytics SDKs, zero advertising SDKs, zero third-party dependencies. No IDFA, no tracking.
  • Transactions, budgets and assets live in the app's own database on your device (Apple SwiftData).
  • Moving to a new iPhone? Share a JSON backup file out yourself and restore from it on the new device.

The details are in the privacy policy.

The rest of the budget app

  • Budgets per category, with what is left clearly shown.
  • Statistics — a donut chart of where the money went, and a trend graph across months.
  • Accounts and cards tracked separately from cash.
  • Recurring expenses posted automatically on a chosen day each month — rent, subscriptions, insurance.
  • Editable categories, named the way you actually think about spending.
  • Backup and restore via a JSON file you control.
  • On-device AI summaries and category suggestions — on iOS 26 and Apple Intelligence supported devices.

Where it works

Receipt and statement formats are recognised for the United States, Germany, South Korea, the Philippines and Japan. The interface is available in English, German, Korean and Filipino, with USD, EUR, KRW and PHP as currencies.

The price

Free. All of it. No in-app purchases, no subscription, no ads, no tracking, and no feature that turns out to be locked later.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to connect my bank?

No, and you could not if you wanted to. Harubi has no bank connection feature. It reads receipts and screenshots instead.

Does it track cash spending?

Yes — this is the main reason to use it. Photograph the receipt and the cash purchase is recorded like any other.

Are my receipt photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Recognition happens on your iPhone and the image is not stored or sent afterwards. The single exception is if you choose to report a misread image to the developer: that opens a pre-filled mail draft that you must send yourself.

What if it reads something wrong?

Correct it on the review screen before saving. Every field is tappable.

Where are my backups kept?

On this device. You can see the list under Settings → Management → ‘Back up & restore your entries’, and they are removed together with Harubi if you delete the app. So use ‘Share a backup’ to save the file to the Files app or email it to yourself. When you change devices or reinstall the app, only a file you have shared can be used.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

Nothing moves across on its own — you take the backup file with you. Share the file from your old iPhone, then tap ‘Restore’ in Harubi on the new one and pick that file; your entries come back as they were. Recurring expenses and installment plans are included. Photos and videos attached to memos are not, and restoring replaces the entries already on that device.

Is there an iPad or Apple Watch version?

Not at the moment. Harubi is iPhone-only and needs iOS 17 or later.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is nothing to buy.
